# Second Blender art pass

The approved image remains the visual target. These are editable 3D renders, not image-generator edits.

## Changes

- Procedural ivory glaze and jade variation, additional clear coat, finer enamel placement.
- Stone color and normal variation.
- Moss modeled as thousands of tapered elements instead of smooth balls.
- Two hero leaves with curved surfaces, central and branching veins, translucent shading.
- Revised tile arrangement, camera, depth of field, warm key and leaf backlight.
- V2 inspection identified clipping, repetitive fern geometry and excessive empty background. V3 reframes the complete pair, hides the ferns and adds weathered background stones.

## Files

- refine_scene.py builds V2 from the original proof scene.
- finish_still.py builds V3 from V2.
- ceramic-garden-v3.blend is the editable scene for further refinement.
- blender-art-v3.png is the 1080×1920 still, 64 Cycles samples with denoising.

## Scope and remaining work

Still-image art pass only. No finished ad or new video is delivered in this pass. The procedural environment remains stylized; it should not be represented as matching the photorealistic approved image. Final material and environment approval is needed before investing in the complete animation. Growth animation, timing, titles, sound and full-video quality control are not yet implemented.
